Mooring is a free theme that makes any store feel like a well-kept shop.

It is classic and warm, and it fits any kind of store that wants to feel welcoming: serif type throughout, a parchment ground with cream cards, a deep navy header and footer, and brass details. Photos sit in picture frames with a mat and a fine inner rule. It works as well for a handful of products as for a few hundred.

**A home page with a welcome.** It opens on your words beside a framed photo with brass corners, with a word or two of the heading in italics if you like. With no photo set, it uses your top category's image. Product rows sit on cream cards with the name and price centered under each photo, under titles set over a hairline with a small diamond.

**Categories in arched frames.** Your categories hang on a shelf, each with its own image in an arch-topped or square frame and its name beneath.

**A note from the shop.** A letter-style section with an italic opening line, a large first letter, your sign-off and an optional framed photo. A navy band of up to four short promises with small icons, and a newsletter sign-up, close the page.

**Category pages like a printed catalog.** Each category opens on a centered title page: its image as a framed banner, its description and links to its subcategories. On wide screens a sidebar lists subcategories and filters beside a three-column grid. On phones the filters fold away, so shoppers land on the products.

**A product page with room to decide.** The photo sits on a framed mat with the other photos as small framed thumbnails beside it. The details sit on a cream card: name, price, options, stock, subscriptions, quantity, the products that pair with it and your own text. The description, a bundle's contents, the product's attributes and your notes for every product fold neatly below.

**Your look, without code.** Set fonts, type size, spacing, page width, buttons and cards for the whole store in Wave's Theme tab, give the home page's sections their own background, text and accent colors, or ask the agent to recolor the whole store. Store-wide choices are set once: a sticky header, a second photo on hover, color dots, sale and sold-out tags, an add button on cards and the quantity box.

**Every page, built in.** Like every Wave theme, Mooring lays out every Swell page type: home, category, product, search, cart, account, blog, content pages, policies and more. The cart opens in a drawer, and customers can track orders, buy again and manage subscriptions. The header and footer are shared by every page, and every fixed word shoppers see is yours to reword in Store text.

**Edit it in Wave.** Click any section or line of text to change it, and move, hide or add sections. Or ask Wave's AI agent in plain words; when Mooring has no section for what you want, it writes one in its style. Grids, categories, menus and the blog read your Swell data, so new products appear on their own.

**Free.** Mooring costs nothing to install, customize or publish.

**Good to know.** Give your categories images: the shelf and category pages use them. Sections that need your words (the shop note and the promises) stay hidden until you fill them. Mooring is a general theme, so it has no lookbook, mega menu or sections made for one kind of store. There is no photo zoom, quick view or sticky add-to-cart bar, and the header has a search button rather than a search box. Star ratings appear only with a reviews app installed.
